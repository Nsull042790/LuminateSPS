The Story

A modern lowcountry estate

Set behind a canopy of century oaks, 1850 Benton Boulevard pairs classic Savannah proportion with a light-filled modern plan. Ten-foot ceilings run the full main level, a chef's kitchen anchors the great room, and pocketing glass walls open the living space to a resort pool and summer kitchen. The primary wing offers a private terrace, spa bath, and boutique dressing room.
