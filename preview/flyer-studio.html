You liked the LumiListing Vision site — this round brings its exact design language to print: navy atmosphere with soft glows, glass stat chips, bento photography, the gold-on-navy price moment, floating overlap cards. Fully on-brand: official palette (≤2 secondaries per piece), real embedded brand fonts, correct logo colorways, official FDIC/EHL badges, full disclaimer on every footer.
