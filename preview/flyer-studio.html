THE LUMINATE LIST

Passaic County EditionVol. 21 · No. 443667West Milford, New Jersey

A storybook cape, reimagined for modern life

Behind the oaks on Louis Street, a rebuilt classic pairs cottage charm with a crisp contemporary interior — and a backyard built for staying home.

Turn off Union Valley Road and the lane narrows, the canopy closes, and number twenty-one appears the way good houses should: white siding, steep gable, flag on the porch. Inside, the surprise — hardwood floors run a sunlit, open main level where the white shaker kitchen anchors everything, finished with stainless appliances, picket-tile backsplash, and workspace enough for two cooks and a homework station.

Upstairs is the showpiece: a vaulted primary retreat tucked under the rafters, wood headboard wall glowing between sconces, with a spa bath alongside — double vanity, framed mirrors, and a walk-in shower behind printed linen.

"A vaulted retreat hides under the rafters — the kind of room buyers remember."
The rebuilt cape at 21 Louis Street

Out back, the property earns its keep: a level fire-pit lawn ringed by Adirondack chairs, a paver patio for long dinners, and a detached garage with loft — studio, workshop, or the world's best home office.

White shaker kitchen, stainless suite

West Milford is Passaic County's lake country — state forest on three sides, trail networks, weekend water, and commuter routes that put the city within reach. Louis Street sits minutes from town shopping and schools.
